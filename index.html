Common questions

Things people are asking.

Who can join?
Anyone who works in L&D: leaders, managers, specialists, instructional designers, consultants and freelancers. We're starting out strongest in the US and UK, but everyone's welcome.
What do I share, and who sees it?
A short profile of your company's L&D function: team size, budget, priorities, and the tools you use with a quick rating. Anyone else who's shared can see it, alongside every other company. Your name is never attached to it.
Am I anonymous?
Yes. Your name is never tied to what you share. Companies are named, but pay and budgets only ever show as ranges, and only once a few people from a company have added theirs.
What does it cost?
Free to share and free to read. Later on, paid tiers will be for recruiters and vendors who want more, never for the L&D community itself.
What if I'm not sure I can share this?
What we ask for isn't confidential: broad structure, the tools you use, rough budget ranges, general priorities. Share what you're comfortable with. Partial profiles are welcome.
Can I do it now, and when do I see other profiles?
Yes, you can share right now. A company's profile opens up once three people from it have added theirs, and you'll be first to know when it does. It fills out fast once people get going.
