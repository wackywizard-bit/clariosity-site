How the give-to-get works

Give once. Gain access for the year.

A simple exchange: peer-contributed data only stays peer-contributed if everyone in the community gives a little. Here's what that looks like in practice.

01

What you give

One structured profile of your company's L&D function. Fields include:

  • Company name, industry, headcount band, location
  • L&D team size and reporting line
  • Approximate L&D spend per employee (banded)
  • Your pay — base and bonus, in bands
  • Your team's top priorities this year
  • Vendors and tools used, with satisfaction ratings
  • Optional: open roles, culture notes
02

What you get

Founding contributor status. When Clariosity launches, you receive:

  • First to be notified as company profiles go live
  • Read access to every contributing company's profile
  • Full filter and search — by industry, size, geography, tooling
  • Peer benchmarking — see where your function sits
  • Vendor satisfaction ratings, in aggregate
03

How access works at launch

Once the platform is live:

  • You'll receive an individual account tied to your contributor email
  • Refresh your profile annually to keep access
  • Changed jobs? Submit a new profile for your new company
  • Withdraw your contribution any time
  • No paid tiers for individual L&D pros — ever
Anonymity & trust

Your contribution is protected.

The anonymity model is the central design decision of Clariosity. Three things you should know:

Anonymous at the individual level Your name is never tied to any profile you submit. We verify contributors privately to maintain data quality, but no one else ever sees who contributed an individual entry.
Sensitive data shown in bands Specific salary figures, exact budgets, and similar fields appear only as ranges. And only once multiple peers from the same company have contributed — single-contributor companies show only structural information.
You stay in control Withdraw your contribution at any time and we'll remove it within 7 days. Update your profile whenever your function changes. No data is sold. No data is shared with third parties without your explicit consent.

Common questions

Things people ask before contributing.

What if my company hasn't given me permission to share?
The data we collect is non-confidential information that doesn't typically require explicit authorization — functional structure, vendor names in use, broad budget ranges, public-facing priorities. If you're unsure about anything specific, share what you're comfortable with. Partial profiles are welcome and still valuable.
How do you verify contributions are accurate?
In the early phase, every contribution is reviewed by hand. We verify contributors through the email you provide — and, optionally, a LinkedIn URL — kept private and never published, to confirm you genuinely work at (or recently worked at) the company you're contributing about. Once we have multiple contributors per company, we cross-reference for consistency.
When will I get access?
Clariosity is currently being built — we're targeting a limited beta in Q3 2026 with broader access by year-end. There's no waitlist: you can contribute now, and each company's profile publishes once three peers from that company have contributed. As a founding contributor you're first to be notified as profiles go live, and once the platform is live you'll have an individual account to sign in and browse the database.
What happens if I leave my company?
Your existing contribution stays in the database (it represents your company's L&D function at the time you contributed). When you start at a new company, you're welcome to submit a new profile for the new company to keep your access fresh. Or you can withdraw your existing contribution at any time and we'll remove it within 7 days.
Will my contribution ever be sold?
No. Individual contributions are never sold to third parties. Long-term, Clariosity will offer paid tiers to recruiters and L&D vendors for access to aggregated, anonymised insights — never raw individual contributions. The L&D community remains the customer the platform is built for.
Can my company claim or respond to its profile?
Yes — eventually. Once we're out of beta, employers will be able to claim their company's profile and add an official statement. They cannot edit or remove individual contributions. This keeps the platform honest and useful while giving companies a voice.
